# 📇 Sahil Ansari – Digital Contact Card

A premium, Apple-keynote-style bento contact card built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Hosted via GitHub Pages and accessible through a QR code embedded in my email signature.

🔗 **Live:** [sahilansari0797.github.io/contact-card](https://sahilansari0797.github.io/contact-card/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Bento grid** — 12 purposeful tiles on a golden-ratio rhythm (top band : bottom row = 1.618 : 1), each fact appearing exactly once
- **iPhone-unlock entrance** — tiles fly in from all directions with spring physics; the hero monogram blooms from center first
- **Holographic "SA" hero** — breathing teal–indigo gradient on a deep navy tile, glows brighter on hover
- **Smart StandBy clock** — live Riyadh time with an availability status (working hours Sun–Thu, 9:00–18:00)
- **Glass chips** — liquid-glass squircles throughout: hand-drawn SVG country flags on the phone tiles, clickable email icons, and the company wordmark in a dark lozenge
- **Save Contact** — one tap downloads a complete `.vcf` (both numbers, both emails, LinkedIn, title, company)
- **WhatsApp quick chat** — wa.me buttons on both numbers, opens the app directly on mobile
- **Click-to-copy** — tap any email tile to copy the address, with a toast confirmation
- **Directions** — opens Google Maps routing from the viewer's location to the Wedo Solutions office
- **Light & dark mode** — follows the system theme; accent tiles stay saturated in the dark
- **Fully responsive** — 12-column grid on desktop, two columns on tablet, single column on phones

## 🚀 How It Works

1. QR code is embedded in the email signature
2. Recipient scans the QR → lands on this hosted contact card
3. They tap **Save Contact** to download the `.vcf` and add me to their contacts instantly
